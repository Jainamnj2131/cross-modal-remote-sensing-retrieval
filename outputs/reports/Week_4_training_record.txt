Training Execution
------------------

Initial training attempt:
Batch size 64 caused CUDA Out Of Memory on the 4 GB RTX 3050.

Resolution:
Reduced physical batch size to 16 and enabled 16-bit mixed precision.

Data-loading optimization:
Initial num_workers=0 resulted in approximately 1.0 it/s.
Changed num_workers to 4.

After optimization:
Approximately 4.1–4.4 it/s.

This reduced the approximate epoch duration from around 21–22 minutes
to approximately 5–6 minutes per epoch.
